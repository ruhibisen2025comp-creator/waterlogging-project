How Pune WaterGuard Works

📍

Location

The entered Pune locality is converted into geographic coordinates.

🌧️

Weather

Current precipitation, rainfall and humidity are retrieved for the location.

⛰️

Terrain

Elevation and the available terrain input are prepared for the model.

🤖

ML Prediction

The trained Random Forest model calculates a waterlogging risk probability.

🗺️

Risk Map

Historical Pune hotspots and the searched location are displayed on the map.

⚠️

Risk Awareness

The result is a decision-support estimate and not a guarantee of live road conditions.
