ABOUT THE SYSTEM

How Pune WaterGuard Works

📍

Location

The selected Pune locality is resolved to coordinates for analysis.

🌧️

Weather

Current precipitation, rainfall and humidity are retrieved for the selected location.

⛰️

Terrain

Terrain elevation and a local elevation-based slope estimate are used as model inputs.

🤖

ML Risk

The trained Random Forest model returns a waterlogging probability and risk category.

🗺️

Historical Hotspots

Available Pune hotspot records are displayed on the interactive map.

⚠️

Interpretation

The result is a risk estimate and should not be treated as a guarantee of present road conditions.
